I / PROPOSITION

The image begins where sound disappears.

Range Echo begins with a refusal: sound should not be illustrated.

The work does not seek a faithful diagram of frequency, nor does it turn music into a decorative sequence of peaks. Instead, it asks what remains after sound has already passed through the body. Pressure becomes curvature. Rhythm becomes recurrence. A transient becomes a bruise of light.

What appears on screen is a field suspended between surface and atmosphere. It has no horizon in the conventional sense, yet it behaves as if it possesses gravity, weather and memory.

声音不是信息，而是一种留下痕迹的力量。

NOT A VISUALISATION OF MUSIC, BUT A MEMORY OF ITS PRESSURE.
II

THE CHARGED ABSENCE

The centre is not a subject.

The centre of Range Echo is deliberately left unoccupied by interface, image or symbol. It is a charged absence: a site waiting to be disturbed.

Pressure gathers there before moving outwards in concentric waves. The gesture resembles a surface remembering that it has been touched. Each ring is born from an event, yet survives it briefly, carrying the event beyond its source.

The source itself often remains invisible. A deep impact is never represented by an object falling into the field. Only its consequence is given form: compression, recoil, a white edge, a temporary inversion of space. An unseen force becomes more present precisely because it withholds its image.

中心不是主体，而是等待发生的空缺。

III / A MATERIAL GRAMMAR

Colour is treated as a condition of perception, not a code.

BLACK / DISTANCE

Black is not a background.

It is the interval before an event and the residue after it. It lets the field recede beyond the screen, giving every point a particular loneliness.

黑色是距离。

MAGENTA / AFTERIMAGE

Magenta is what the eye refuses to release.

It appears less as local colour than as retinal persistence: a stain of energy that remains after attention has moved elsewhere.

洋红是余像。

WHITE / THRESHOLD

White exists only at the limit.

It arrives when energy can no longer remain colour. Its scarcity turns overexposure into an event rather than an effect.

白色只属于临界点。

IV

LISTENING WITH THE SKIN

Frequency becomes a form of bodily address.

The lower register has weight. It bends the apparent ground and enlarges the scale of the image. The middle register gives the field articulation, allowing pressure to travel rather than merely rise. High frequencies behave as brief directional cuts—trajectories that flare and disappear before they can become objects.

Tempo establishes a climate rather than issuing a command. Rotation, recurrence and breath remain slightly disobedient to the beat, because complete obedience would turn listening into measurement.

There is movement even in silence. The dormant field breathes just enough to suggest that sound has not created the space, only awakened it.

低频有重量，高频有速度；静默也仍然呼吸。

V

THE WITHDRAWN INTERFACE

The interface knows when to disappear.

It occupies the margins because the centre belongs to sensation. Typography remains as a fixed mass against the unstable field: excessive, blunt, unwilling to dissolve into frictionless digital ambience.

Controls withdraw until summoned. This is not function hidden beneath beauty, but an ordering of attention: encounter the work first, then decide whether to intervene.

界面退到边缘，把中央还给感知。

VI

THE WITHDRAWN MEDIUM

Technology should leave before perception does.

Computation is present everywhere, but it is denied the status of spectacle. It is the material condition through which an immaterial event acquires temporary weight.

The mechanism succeeds when it recedes long enough for the image to be felt as weather, pressure or memory. Economy prevents excess from becoming style and forces every luminous event to justify its presence.

技术让感知发生，然后主动退场。

VII

TIME AFTER THE EVENT

Nothing is allowed to become fully stable.

A ring expands until its edge loses definition. An impact survives only as recoil. A trajectory vanishes before it can be followed to a conclusion. The work is composed less from objects than from arrivals and disappearances.

Sound is always already leaving us. To listen is to experience presence becoming memory in real time. Range Echo gives that transition a spatial body, however provisional.

作品呈现感受正在成为记忆的瞬间。

VIII / CLOSING NOTE

Range Echo is neither an instrument nor a picture.

It is a temporary climate assembled between vibration, attention and afterimage.

It asks the viewer to remain with what cannot be held: an impact without an object, a centre without a subject, a light produced by something that has already disappeared.

What we see is not sound.
It is the space sound leaves behind.

我们看见的不是声音，而是声音离开后留下的空间。
