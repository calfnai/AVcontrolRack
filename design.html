NOTES TOWARDS AN AUDITORY IMAGE

RANGE
ECHO

Audibility does not become image.

It returns as evidence of a force already withdrawn.

可听之物不被图像化，只留下退场后的证据。

ABSENCE IMPACT AFTERIMAGE RETURN

I / PROPOSITION

The image is not sound made visible. It is perception caught revising itself.

Range Echo begins from a disagreement with the regime of visualisation: the assumption that sound, once rendered visible, should become legible.

Legibility is never innocent. It converts duration into object, intensity into quantity, and listening into the recognition of a pre-arranged sign. The work withholds this conversion. It does not explain the event; it constructs the conditions under which its consequences may be encountered.

What appears is therefore neither transcription nor metaphor. It is a field in which sensation remains unresolved: spatial enough to be inhabited, unstable enough to resist becoming an illustration.

My concern is not fidelity but authority: what permits a visual trace to stand in for an event that cannot be recovered?

作品拒绝把声音整理成一个容易读懂的对象。

THE EVENT IS WITHHELD. ITS CONSEQUENCES ARE NOT.
II

THE CHARGED ABSENCE

The centre operates as a withheld cause.

The centre is deliberately denied an object. It is not a compositional vacancy awaiting content, but a causal position whose authority depends upon remaining unseen.

Concentric movement begins there and immediately exceeds it. Cause is displaced into propagation; origin becomes readable only through delay. The field offers no event in itself, only an expanding set of relations through which an event may be inferred.

This asymmetry between an absent source and excessive evidence is the work's central proposition. Visibility is assigned to consequence rather than cause. The image ceases to certify what happened and becomes the unstable site at which happening is retrospectively produced.

中心隐藏原因，只让后果向外扩散。

III / A MATERIAL GRAMMAR

Colour distributes perceptual authority; it does not describe intensity.

BLACK / DISTANCE

Black establishes the limit of available evidence.

It refuses the fiction that the entire field is available to sight. What cannot be distinguished remains structurally present rather than visually completed.

黑色是距离。

MAGENTA / AFTERIMAGE

Magenta marks persistence without identity.

It identifies no object. It registers the duration for which an event continues to condition perception after its source can no longer be located.

洋红是余像。

WHITE / THRESHOLD

White interrupts the field as a limit condition.

It appears where differentiation briefly collapses. Its scarcity prevents emphasis from becoming atmosphere and intensity from becoming decoration.

白色只属于临界点。

IV

LISTENING WITH THE SKIN

Frequency is organised as differential pressure, not pictorial information.

The lower register has weight. It bends the apparent ground and enlarges the scale of the image. The middle register gives the field articulation, allowing pressure to travel rather than merely rise. High frequencies behave as brief directional cuts—trajectories that flare and disappear before they can become objects.

Tempo establishes a climate rather than issuing a command. Rotation, recurrence and breath remain slightly disobedient to the beat, because complete obedience would turn listening into measurement.

There is movement even in silence. The dormant field breathes just enough to suggest that sound has not created the space, only awakened it.

低频有重量，高频有速度；静默也仍然呼吸。

V

THE WITHDRAWN INTERFACE

The interface is a protocol for distributing attention.

Its location at the margin is not a gesture of visual modesty. It determines which operations remain continuously legible and which must be temporarily suspended for the field to retain perceptual priority.

Controls withdraw until summoned. This is not function hidden beneath beauty, but an ordering of attention: encounter the work first, then decide whether to intervene.

界面退到边缘，把中央还给感知。

VI

THE WITHDRAWN MEDIUM

The apparatus is present as a condition and absent as an image.

Computation is not concealed; it is refused representational privilege. The apparatus organises what can appear without becoming the content of that appearance.

Constraint is consequently methodological rather than merely economical. It limits the number of possible gestures so that each visible event remains a decision, not an automatic demonstration of capacity.

技术让感知发生，然后主动退场。

VII

TIME AFTER THE EVENT

The work occupies the delay between occurrence and recognition.

A ring expands until its edge loses definition. An impact survives only as recoil. A trajectory vanishes before it can be followed to a conclusion. The work is composed less from objects than from arrivals and disappearances.

The event is never simply present to perception. By the time it is recognised, it has already been reorganised by attention, expectation and recall. Range Echo does not overcome that delay. It gives the delay duration, scale and consequence.

作品停留在发生与被理解之间。

VIII / CLOSING NOTE

Range Echo stages an epistemic delay between vibration and recognition.

It refuses the promise that an image can make an auditory event fully available.

To render is also to reorganise: to select a duration, establish a hierarchy and decide which consequences will count as evidence. The work makes that operation perceptible without pretending to resolve it. Its subject is not sound, but the authority claimed by every image that says: this is what happened.

THE EVENT WITHDRAWS.
ITS CONSEQUENCES REMAIN.

事件退场，后果仍在。
